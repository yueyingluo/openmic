My work builds on Chen Shouwen's TTS pipeline. I turn the coach's performance cues into controls that the program can execute.

First, I take a script with tags. The compiler turns the tags into Speech and Pause events. The execution layer sends expression instructions to the speech model and inserts silence between the returned audio segments.

In the compiler, I use two frozen dataclasses. Speech stores the text for one spoken segment and its controls. Pause stores the duration of silence to be inserted locally.

Based on the tags and the selected condition, the program chooses the text sent to the model, the instruction, and the speaking speed. For example, the words marked with [EMPHASIS] in this sentence become the model's <strong> markup.

Sometimes, one sentence ran straight into the next. So I added silence between the segments to control the gaps.
